Pedagogical Philosophy

Why PaperMap?

Traditional research papers are written for reviewers, not learners. PaperMap redesigns them for human intuition.

🕹️

Playable Simulations

Every paper comes with interactive calculators, architecture simulators, and visual matrix sandboxes.

🔬

Deep Dive Labs

Expert sections that scrutinize the hidden math, engineering edge-cases, and empirical trade-offs.

🗺️

Structured Curriculum

Papers are organized sequentially so that each milestone answers the open problems left by the preceding one.

⚡

Pure Vanilla Web

Zero node modules, zero build pipelines, and zero framework overhead. Instant load times and guaranteed longevity.

📝

Mastery Quizzes

5-question interactive quizzes with instant explanatory feedback to test your conceptual retention.

🤝

Open Contribution

Rigorous, standardized style guidelines that enable any AI researcher to add new interactive guides.
